A clear path through the work

From account connection to conversation.

The intended workflow is simple. Actual availability depends on the current product release and the permissions available to your business.

Install on a supported desktop

Choose Windows, macOS, or Linux when a verified release is published for your platform. At present, downloads are not published.

Authorize eligible business accounts

Connect using the official Meta authorization flow supported by the app. Only connect accounts you own or are explicitly authorized to manage. Never share your Meta password.

Add your business knowledge

Configure products, prices, offers, FAQs, and policies so AI-assisted responses can use relevant business-provided context.

Manage conversations with control

Use AI assistance for appropriate common questions and switch a conversation to human handling when information is uncertain or judgment is needed.

Account eligibility, app configuration, granted permissions, Meta review, messaging restrictions, and platform policies affect which connections and features are available. This website does not grant Meta API access or indicate app approval.

Keep the human close.

AI output can be incomplete or incorrect. Review consequential replies and take over when a customer needs a person.
