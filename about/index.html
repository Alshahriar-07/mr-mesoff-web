About the product

One desktop workflow for customer conversations.

MR MESOFF is a desktop-first workspace being designed for businesses and teams that manage conversations across supported messaging channels.

Why it exists

Businesses often need to check separate inboxes to keep up with customer messages. MR MESOFF aims to bring supported conversations from WhatsApp Business, Instagram Professional, and Facebook Messenger into one focused desktop workflow.

Business context, not guesswork

A business can provide information such as products, prices, offers, FAQs, and policies. AI-assisted replies are intended to use that context for common questions rather than inventing details. If required information is missing or uncertain, the response should be deferred to a human instead of making up a price, stock status, policy, or promise.

AI and human handling

The planned workflow includes a conversation-level choice between AI-assisted handling and human handling. A person can take over when a conversation calls for empathy, judgment, sensitive information, or a decision. AI output can still be incomplete or incorrect, so consequential information should be reviewed before it is sent or relied on.

Offers and messaging

Products, offers, and campaign workflows may help teams organize customer communication. Sending messages is subject to recipient consent, applicable channel permissions, messaging windows and template requirements, and current platform policies.

Connected business accounts

Businesses should connect only accounts they own or are explicitly authorized to manage. Connections are intended to use official Meta authorization flows; MR MESOFF should never ask users to provide a Meta password. Account eligibility and available permissions vary by account type and setup.

Availability and limitations

Specific capabilities depend on the current application release, configuration, operating system, Meta permissions, account eligibility, app review, and platform rules. The presence of a channel on this website does not mean that an integration or permission is currently available, approved, or production-ready. No download, subscription checkout, or Meta approval is represented as live here.
