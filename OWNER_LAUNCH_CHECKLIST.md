# MR MESOFF Website Owner Launch Checklist

This checklist is for the product owner. The public website intentionally does not publish invented answers for these items.

## Legal and contact
- [ ] Replace the legal operator placeholder with the verified legal or business identity.
- [ ] Configure a real, monitored support and privacy email.
- [ ] Add any legally required business address and contact details.
- [ ] Set the actual effective and review dates.
- [ ] Complete jurisdiction-specific Terms fields and obtain legal review.
- [ ] Verify Privacy Policy statements against deployed storage, logs, backups, analytics, hosting, and retention behavior.
- [ ] Confirm the deletion and disconnect flow, including what happens to backups and retained records.

## Product and integrations
- [ ] Confirm which product features and operating systems are actually released.
- [ ] Verify Meta OAuth or Embedded Signup flows, redirect URIs, requested permissions, and review status.
- [ ] Confirm actual AI runtime, external providers, prompts/context shared, and user/admin controls.
- [ ] Implement, secure, deploy, and test any Meta deletion callback before configuring it.
- [ ] Confirm that business accounts are authorized and that messaging controls follow consent and platform rules.

## Downloads and pricing
- [ ] Publish real Windows, macOS, and Linux release artifacts and configure their URLs.
- [ ] Publish and test `install.ps1` and `install.sh`; verify HTTPS, error handling, and checksums/signatures where available.
- [ ] Add verified versions, release notes, and platform requirements.
- [ ] Confirm whether $20/month and $199/year remain planned or are active.
- [ ] Before paid checkout launches, publish accurate provider, billing, tax, cancellation, and refund terms.
- [ ] Explain any separate Meta usage charges accurately.

## Brand and publication
- [ ] Add a real creator portfolio URL only if the creator wants to publish one.
- [ ] Check all public routes, metadata, links, and deployment routing on the production host.
- [ ] Review all public legal text and integration statements before requesting platform review.
