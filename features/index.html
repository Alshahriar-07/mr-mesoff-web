Designed for customer conversations

Useful context. Clear control.

MR MESOFF is being designed to bring supported business messaging channels into one desktop workflow. Availability depends on the release, account eligibility, permissions, and platform rules.

01 / MESSAGES

Unified inbox

Review supported conversations from connected WhatsApp Business, Instagram Professional, and Facebook Messenger accounts in one workspace.

02 / HUMAN CONTROL

Conversation-level handling

The workflow is designed to let a business switch an individual conversation between AI-assisted and human handling.

03 / BUSINESS CONTEXT

Grounded assistance

Products, prices, offers, FAQs, and policies supplied by the business can inform answers. The assistant should not invent missing facts.

04 / ESCALATION

Defer when uncertain

When information is incomplete or a question needs judgment, the intended behavior is to route the conversation to a person.

05 / ORGANIZATION

Offers and campaigns

Organize offers and messaging workflows. Sending remains subject to consent, channel permissions, message windows, templates, and platform policies.

06 / CONNECTIONS

Authorized accounts

Connect only accounts you own or have permission to manage, through official authorization flows. Never provide a Meta password to MR MESOFF.

These describe product goals, not a claim that each capability is available in a released build. Confirm feature availability against the current release before relying on it.

Make the handoff explicit

AI can assist. People make the call.

Important business, legal, pricing, inventory, or policy details should be reviewed. If the system does not have a reliable answer, a human should take over.
