1. Overview

This draft describes categories of information that may be processed when using the MR MESOFF website and desktop application. The final policy must match the deployed product and its actual data flows. MR MESOFF is independent and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Meta Platforms, Inc.

2. Information that may be processed

Depending on the features enabled and permissions granted, the application may process:

  • Account identifiers and profile information used for authentication.
  • Business account, Page, Instagram account, and WhatsApp Business identifiers made available through authorized integrations.
  • Messages, message metadata, and attachments where the platform and granted permissions permit access.
  • Customer or contact information and conversation history.
  • Business-provided products, prices, FAQs, offers, and policies.
  • Technical diagnostics, application logs, and error reports if collected by the deployed product.

The operator must remove categories the product does not process and add any actual categories omitted here before publication.

Depending on implemented features, information may be used for authentication, connecting authorized channels, displaying or sending messages, AI-assisted replies, storing business context, customer support, service operation, troubleshooting, abuse prevention, and compliance with applicable obligations. These are possible purposes, not confirmation that each function is currently active.
