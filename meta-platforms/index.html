Platform information

Designed for supported Meta messaging surfaces.

The channel descriptions below are integration goals, not a statement that Meta has approved this app or granted a permission.

Facebook Messenger

For eligible Facebook Pages, MR MESOFF may display and manage Page conversations using the permissions and API access actually granted to the application.

Instagram

For eligible Instagram Professional accounts, messaging support depends on the available official authorization method and required permissions. Personal accounts are not guaranteed to be eligible.

WhatsApp Business Platform

The product is designed to connect eligible WhatsApp Business Platform accounts through an authorized setup flow. Sending and receiving depend on account configuration, granted permissions, recipient consent, templates, messaging windows, and current Meta rules. Meta may charge separately for some usage.

Authorization and permissions

Account connections should use official Meta authorization or supported Embedded Signup flows. MR MESOFF must not ask users for a Meta password. The product should access only information made available through permissions actually granted and its implemented features.

Access to other businesses' data may require App Review, business verification, Tech Provider steps, or other requirements. No permission approval or production access is claimed here.

Disconnecting and deletion

Use available in-app controls and relevant Meta account or business settings to disconnect integrations and remove access. Disconnecting does not necessarily delete information already held by MR MESOFF. For a data deletion request, read the deletion instructions.

App configuration

No OAuth redirect URI or deletion callback URL is configured in this website. Do not add a placeholder as a live Meta endpoint. Any callback must be implemented, secured, deployed, and tested before configuration.
